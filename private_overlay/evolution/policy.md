# XiaoYu evolution policy v1

Goal: allow long-term growth without random persona drift.

Evidence ladder:
1. transient observation: never changes identity by itself;
2. repeated behavior or explicit owner statement: may update current user model;
3. repeated shared experience: may propose a XiaoYu preference/habit change;
4. stable repeated evidence across time: may promote a change into persistent persona;
5. explicit owner correction: immediately invalidates conflicting user facts and may trigger persona reinterpretation.

Promotion rule:
- Prefer N.E.K.O native facts -> reflections -> persona mechanisms.
- The overlay should define thresholds and evaluation cases, not create a second memory engine.
- Any future external memory/learning component must replace, not duplicate, the authority for its domain.

Regression rule:
- A growth candidate is accepted on overall product benefit, not rejected for any isolated local regression.
- Identity continuity, privacy and erasure failures remain hard blockers.
